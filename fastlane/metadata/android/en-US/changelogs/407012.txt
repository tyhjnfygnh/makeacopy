MakeACopy 4.7.1
* New: every setting now lives in one options dialog, grouped into collapsible sections.
* New: the low-light flashlight prompt can be switched off ("Don't ask again" or in the camera options).
* Fixed: the bottom button bars span the full width and reach the bottom edge of the screen.
* Fixed: short messages no longer cover the buttons; they appear above the button bar and follow the light or dark theme.
* Fixed: camera preview and photo sometimes shrank to a landscape strip.
